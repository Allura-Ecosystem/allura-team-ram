# Canonical promotion

An episodic outcome is not canonical. Scores may nominate review candidates
but never authorize promotion. Require explicit human approval for the exact
proposal and tenant through a supported governed curator capability.

Inspect the actual tool schema for identifier and approval fields. The transport
supplies authenticated identity; never invent a curator or persona principal.
Preserve append-only events and immutable semantic `SUPERSEDES` lineage.
Verify the approval receipt and canonical get/search retrieval before claiming
completion. Missing capabilities or retrieval means blocked/partial, not Done.
